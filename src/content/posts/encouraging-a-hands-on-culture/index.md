As engineers, it's easy to get lost in the loop of daily stand-ups, sprint retrospectives, checking Linear tickets, writing code, and reviewing GitHub pull requests.

We tend to forget _why_ we build in the first place: _problem solving_. This is why at Bukas, I'm one of the proponents of advocating a “hands-on” and collaborative culture in our engineering team.
